Princeton University · Founder, Preface · Chairman, Gwinnett County Public Library · Principal, Magnolia Prospect

Jonathan "JT" Wu is a finance executive and civic innovator advising investors, governments, and institutions on how capital and policy intersect to create durable public value. A Princeton-trained strategist and the founder of Preface, he brings a unique lens to blended finance and public-private partnerships - combining boardroom discipline with community-scale results. As Chairman of Georgia's largest public library system, he has overseen initiatives delivering over 400% ROI on public funds.
